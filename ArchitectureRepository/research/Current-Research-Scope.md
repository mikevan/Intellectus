# Current research scope: establish the foundation first

Decision date: 2026-11-26. Owner direction: concentrate on contemporary understanding before implementing prospective capabilities.

## Current objective

Build reliable, evidence-grounded, implementation-independent knowledge of existing computational behavior. Validate each specialist's contribution rather than presuming that an AI committee improves understanding.

## Future research horizon (deferred)

Understanding may eventually integrate historical states, causal models, present observations, forecasts, counterfactual scenarios and strategic alternatives. Financial records across multiple years are a candidate future application: infer economic relationships, test forecasts against withheld periods and evaluate possible interventions. This is a research hypothesis, not an established capability. Historical, observed, inferred and hypothetical knowledge must remain distinct.

No temporal forecasting, financial forecasting, scenario generation or strategic decision engine is in the current implementation scope.

## Qualification sequence

| Stage | Question | Acceptance evidence |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Behavioral invariance | Different implementations converge under a declared finite observation contract. |
| P2 | Behavioral discrimination | Negative controls remain distinct, including differences outside discovery samples. |
| P3 | Deliberative correction | Contradicted hypotheses are corrected or explicitly withdrawn; no unsupported promotion. |
| P4 | Domain contribution | Independent domain context yields verified incremental knowledge compared with deterministic-only controls. |

P1 and P2 begin with a small deterministic engineering pilot, separate from the frozen RT-5 protocol. Its purpose is to qualify the harness, not to claim semantic understanding. P3 and P4 follow after the observation and verification foundation is stable. Record actual evidence, failures and conclusions. Retain RT-1 through RT-4 and Phase I evidence unchanged. RT-5 remains unexecuted until its distinct experimental prerequisites are satisfied.

Longer-term engineering principle: build expansively to test the concept; reduce the architecture empirically after identifying necessary components.
