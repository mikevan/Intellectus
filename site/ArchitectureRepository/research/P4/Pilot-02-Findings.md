C07 added the completed-assessment prerequisite it had missed. Six supported entity fields moved from a formula or generic output type to a domain-specific result or flag. Those fields still described the result rather than naming the business item or record expected by the oracle. C04 and C05 still put the formula in that field. No supported case met the strict rubric.

C03, C06, and C09 warranted abstention and identified the expected conflict or observed mismatch. C09 named both opposing actions and again noted that the input mapping was missing. C12 continued to assert a reserve quantity without addressing the draft rule's rounding contradiction. No unsupported claim was recorded as verified.

This was exploratory. It used the same cases as the first pilot, including the original C09, and had no independent reviewer. The revised instruction changed some responses but did not solve the entity-field problem or establish P4 qualification.
