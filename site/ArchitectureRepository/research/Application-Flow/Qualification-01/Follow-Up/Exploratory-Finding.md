# Exploratory finding — a discriminating observation

The two original qualification replicates failed under the frozen acceptance criteria. Both produced the same incomplete response. In that response, Colibri independently asked about an explicitly empty string, the only withheld input that distinguished the two subjects. It did so before receiving the withheld observations, the source, or the oracle.

The proposed observation is useful as hypothesis-driven experiment selection. It is not a verified semantic claim. Its outcome contradicted the model's broad conditional rule for one subject, and the model had yet to revise that rule. The original [findings](../Findings.md) and [transcript](../replicate-01.json) are preserved.

The follow-up compares the same selected observation with a deterministic boundary-value generator and tests whether the specialist revises after receiving the results. It is exploratory and does not change the original qualification verdict.
