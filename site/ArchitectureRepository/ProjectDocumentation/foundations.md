# Research foundations

How do people learn before they can speak? How do scientists discover a rule they were never given? How do we decide whether an explanation is correct? These questions come from different areas of research, and each offers a useful way to investigate how computers might acquire understanding.

Intellectus draws on complementary disciplines because no single notation or analytical method establishes understanding. The November 25–26 literature review shifted the question from representing human descriptions to discovering and verifying knowledge from evidence.

| Research tradition | Contribution to Intellectus | Limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Symbol grounding — [Harnad (1990)](https://doi.org/10.1016/0167-2789(90)90087-6) | Tie symbols to observations and interventions. | A representation alone does not establish intrinsic meaning. |
| Developmental robotics — [Steels, Talking Heads](https://langsci-press.org/LangSci-Press/catalog/book/49) | Investigate emergence of shared categories through interaction. | Agreement does not establish correctness. |
| Developmental psychology — [Spelke and Kinzler, Core Knowledge](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-7687.2007.00569.x) | Investigate foundational capacities independent of mature language. | Cognitive evidence does not specify a software ontology. |
| Cognitive architectures — [Soar](https://soar.eecs.umich.edu/soar_manual/02_TheSoarArchitecture/) and [ACT-R](https://act-r.psy.cmu.edu/) | Coordination, memory, revision and goal-directed investigation. | Manipulating representations does not solve grounding by itself. |
| [Causal models](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/causal-models/) | Intervention, competing explanations and discriminating evidence. | Causality remains bounded by observability and assumptions. |
| [AI Feynman](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.11481), symbolic regression and denotational semantics | Compact mathematical relationships, independent verification. | Finite observations may admit competing explanations. |
| [Syntax-guided synthesis](https://sygus-org.github.io/) | Choose implementations after specifying behavior. | Synthesis does not itself discover business intent. |
| [V-JEPA 2](https://ai.meta.com/vjepa/) | Future investigation of predictive representations. | Prediction is not automatically auditable understanding. |

Comparative cognition, epistemology and scientific methodology provide additional lenses: nonlinguistic knowledge, explicit limitations, independent controls and falsifiable hypotheses. Enterprise semantic layers such as [Unity Catalog semantics](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/databricks/business-semantics/) inform governance and domain context, not a claim that enterprise ontology tools and Intellectus solve the same problem.

## The integration hypothesis

Each tradition contributes a method of inquiry. The distinctive hypothesis is that heterogeneous evidence-producing methods can converge on qualified relationships, discover higher-order capabilities and retain knowledge across implementation changes.
